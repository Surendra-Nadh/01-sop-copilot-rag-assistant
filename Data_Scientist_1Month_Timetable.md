## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Consistency > Intensity** — 8 focused hours beat 12 distracted hours
2. **Build in public** — Tweet/post your progress, write about what you learn
3. **Don't get stuck** — If stuck for 30+ min, move on, come back later
4. **Explain out loud** — If you can explain it simply, you understand it
5. **Apply while learning** — Don't wait until the end to apply for jobs
6. **Network** — Join data science Discord servers, LinkedIn groups, local meetups
7. **Sleep & exercise** — Your brain needs rest to consolidate learning

---

> **Remember:** The goal isn't to know everything — it's to demonstrate you can *learn*, *build*, and *communicate*. A portfolio of 3 solid projects + solid fundamentals will get you interviews. Confidence and communication will get you the job.

**Good luck! 🚀**
